Privacy Policy

Mia Home AI (“Mia,” “we,” or “us”) is operated by Chris Sperandeo. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the website.

Information we process

We process your email address and authentication details to provide an account; photos you choose to upload, your written design instructions, and generated makeover images to provide the service; and records of credits, purchases, and saved makeovers. Our hosting and service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and error logs needed to operate and protect the website.

How your photos and designs are used

Uploaded photos and instructions are sent to an AI service provider (OpenAI) to create your requested design. Generated makeovers are saved in private account-linked storage so you can reopen them. Uploaded source photos are processed to fulfill requests; we do not intentionally publish them as a public gallery. Please do not upload photos containing sensitive personal information, people who have not consented, or content you lack permission to use.

Service providers and payments

We use third-party providers to operate Mia, including Supabase for authentication and saved data, Render for hosting, OpenAI for AI processing, and Stripe for payment processing. When checkout is available, Stripe handles payment details; Mia receives transaction information needed to apply credits and support purchases. Each provider may process data under its own privacy terms.

Storage and retention

Account records and saved makeover images may remain stored while the service operates and until they are deleted or removal is requested, subject to legitimate operational, security, or legal retention needs. Technical logs and payment records may have different retention periods. We do not currently offer an automatic in-app account deletion control.
